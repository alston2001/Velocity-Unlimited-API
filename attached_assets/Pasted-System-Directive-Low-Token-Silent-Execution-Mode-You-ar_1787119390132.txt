1. CONCUSSION ASSESSMENT ENGINE (`src/lib/concussionTracker.ts`)
=====================================================================
 
1. Pupil Size & Anisocoria Analysis (TS Computer Vision):
   - Process front-facing camera frame RGBA buffers around eye bounding regions.
   - Detect pupil edge gradients using intensity thresholding on `Float32Array` buffers to estimate left pupil diameter $d_L$ and right pupil diameter $d_R$ in pixels.
   - Calculate Anisocoria Ratio:
     $$\text{Anisocoria \%} = \frac{|d_L - d_R|}{\max(d_L, d_R)} \times 100$$
   - Score Penalty: Anisocoria $> 10\%$ or bilateral hyper-dilation increases Pupil Score Component (0 to 35 points).
 
2. Postural Sway / Balance Stability Test (IMU Sensor Processing):
   - Record 10-second accelerometer time-series during a single-leg or tandem balance stance.
   - Compute root-mean-square (RMS) acceleration variance across $X, Y, Z$ axes:
     $$\text{Sway}_{\text{RMS}} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N} \sum (a_{x,i}^2 + a_{y,i}^2 + a_{z,i}^2)}$$
   - Score Penalty: $\text{Sway}_{\text{RMS}} > 0.4\text{ m/s}^2$ increases Balance Score Component (0 to 35 points).
 
3. Composite Likelihood Score Matrix (0 - 100):
   $$\text{Total Risk Score} = \text{Pupil Score (0-35)} + \text{Balance Score (0-35)} + \text{Cognitive Penalty (0-30)}$$
   - Cognitive Penalty Calculation: Each wrong answer out of 10 adds 3 points to the Cognitive Penalty Component:
     $$\text{Cognitive Penalty} = 30 \times \left(1 - \frac{\text{Correct Answers}}{10}\right)$$
   - Risk Tiers:
     - 0 – 25: Low Risk
     - 26 – 55: Moderate Risk (Professional Review Suggested)
     - 56 – 100: High Likelihood (Immediate Medical Evaluation Recommended)
